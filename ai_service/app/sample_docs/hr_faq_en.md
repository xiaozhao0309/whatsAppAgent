# HR FAQ

## Q: How many days of annual leave do I get?
Employees get 5 days of annual leave after completing 1 year of service, 10 days after 3 years, and 15 days after 5 years. Annual leave is counted per calendar year and cannot be carried over to the next year.

## Q: How do I request personal (unpaid) leave?
Submit a personal leave request in the "Acme Office" app under the **Approvals** module. Fill in the dates and reason, then your direct manager will approve it. Personal leave is unpaid and is deducted from your salary by the day.

## Q: What is the expense reimbursement process?
1. Submit an expense claim in the "Acme Office" app and upload a photo of the invoice.
2. Your direct manager approves it.
3. After finance approves, the money is transferred to your registered payroll card within 3 business days.
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense; late claims are not reimbursed. Any single claim over SGD 1,000 needs an additional VP approval.

## Q: How long is the probation period?
The probation period is 3 months. During probation the salary is 80% of the confirmed salary. Employees who perform satisfactorily are automatically confirmed at the end of probation.

## Q: What are the standard working hours?
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 - 18:00, with a one-hour lunch break. Overtime on weekdays is paid at 1.5x the hourly rate; weekend and public-holiday overtime is paid at 2x.

## Q: How do I apply for sick leave?
Notify your direct manager on the same day and submit a medical certificate in the "Acme Office" app within 2 working days. Up to 14 days of paid sick leave per year are covered with a valid medical certificate.
