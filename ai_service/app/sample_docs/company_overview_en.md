# Acme Corp — Company Overview

Acme Corp is a SaaS company founded in 2015 and headquartered in Singapore. We build collaboration and productivity software used by teams across Asia-Pacific.

## Q: What does Acme Corp do?
We make three main products:
- **Acme Office**: team chat and collaboration platform with docs, calendar, and video meetings.
- **Acme Drive**: enterprise file storage and sharing with permission controls and version history.
- **Acme Meet**: video conferencing that supports up to 500 participants per meeting.

## Q: Where is the company located?
Our headquarters is in Singapore, with regional offices in Sydney, Bangalore, and Tokyo.
