# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

This handbook summarizes the day-to-day policies and expectations that apply to all employees at Acme Corp. It is meant as a practical guide, not an exhaustive legal contract. When a topic is not covered here, refer to the full policy on the intranet or ask your manager.

## Working Hours and Attendance

Standard office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM SGT, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, whether working from the office or remotely. Outside of core hours, schedules are flexible as long as the work gets done and team commitments are met.

If you are going to be late or absent, notify your direct manager before the start of the working day. Unexplained absences are recorded and may affect performance reviews.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Acme Corp supports a hybrid work model. Employees may work from home up to three days per week, subject to their team's coverage needs. Fully remote arrangements require manager and HR approval and are reviewed every six months.

When working remotely, employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and being reachable through Acme Office during core hours. A home-office stipend of SGD 300 per year is available to cover ergonomic equipment; submit receipts through the expense system.

## Leave Types

In addition to annual leave, the company offers sick leave, personal leave, compassionate leave, and parental leave. Sick leave of up to 14 days per year is paid with a valid medical certificate submitted within two working days. Compassionate leave of up to three paid days is granted for the death of an immediate family member. Parental leave follows the statutory requirements of Singapore, with eligible employees entitled to government-paid leave and an additional two weeks of company-paternity leave for primary caregivers.

All leave requests are submitted through the Approvals module in the Acme Office app and require approval from the direct manager. During peak periods, managers may ask employees to reschedule leave to ensure adequate coverage.

## Expense Reimbursement

Work-related expenses are reimbursed when supported by an original invoice or receipt. Submit claims in the Acme Office app within 30 days of incurring the expense; claims submitted after 30 days are generally not reimbursed. The finance team processes approved claims every Wednesday and transfers the amount to the registered payroll card within three business days.

Any single expense above SGD 1,000 requires an additional vice-president approval before it is paid. Travel and client-entertainment expenses should include a short note describing the business purpose and attendees.

## IT and Data Security

Employees are issued a company account that provides access to email, Acme Office, Acme Drive, and internal systems. Keep your password confidential and enable two-factor authentication when prompted. Do not share your account with anyone, including family members.

Company data must be stored in Acme Drive, not on personal devices or unapproved cloud services. If you suspect a device is lost, stolen, or compromised, report it to IT within 24 hours so access can be revoked. Connect to the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office network.

## Code of Conduct

Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying of any kind are not tolerated and should be reported to HR or through the anonymous whistleblower channel. Confidential information—including customer data, unreleased product plans, and financial results—must not be discussed outside the company or on personal social media accounts.

Employees must avoid conflicts of interest. Outside employment, advisory roles, or significant financial interests in competitors or vendors must be disclosed to HR. Gifts from vendors above SGD 100 in value must be declined or reported.

## Health and Wellbeing

The company provides an annual health screening benefit and access to an employee assistance program offering confidential counseling. If work-related stress is affecting your health, speak with your manager or HR early; adjustments to workload or deadlines can often be arranged.

The Singapore office is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and includes a pantry, quiet rooms, and a shower facility. Employees are encouraged to take regular breaks and to disconnect outside working hours whenever possible.

## Getting Help

For IT issues, submit a ticket through the IT Service module in the Acme Office app; urgent issues are responded to within 30 minutes. For HR questions, email people@acme-corp.example or message the HR team on Acme Office. For payroll or expense questions, contact finance at finance@acme-corp.example.
