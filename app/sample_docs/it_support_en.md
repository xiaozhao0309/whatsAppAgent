# IT Support FAQ

## Q: How do I connect to the company VPN?
1. Download the "Acme VPN" client from the company intranet (Windows and macOS only).
2. Sign in with your employee ID and domain password.
3. Once connected you can access internal systems.
The VPN has daily maintenance from 2:00 - 4:00 AM and cannot be connected during that window. If you forget your password, reset it on the IT service portal.

## Q: I forgot my email password. What should I do?
Go to it.acme-corp.example/reset, verify with your employee ID, and reset it yourself. After resetting you must sign in to your email once with the new password within 10 minutes, otherwise you will need to reset it again.

## Q: My computer is broken. Who do I contact?
Submit an IT ticket: in the "Acme Office" app go to **IT Service** and choose **Fault Report**, then describe the problem. IT response time is within 30 minutes for urgent issues and within 4 hours for normal issues.

## Q: How do I get permission to install software?
Common software can be self-installed under **IT Service → Software Request**. Professional software (such as Adobe or JetBrains products) requires manager approval and is then installed remotely by IT.

## Q: How do I connect to the office Wi-Fi?
Select the "Acme-Staff" network and sign in with your domain account. Guests can use the "Acme-Guest" network by requesting a one-time code from reception.
